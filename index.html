Omnis for Android

Focus, made into a climb.

Block the apps and sites that pull you away, keep your streak crystal lit, and rise through new worlds one session at a time.

Coming soon toGoogle Play See how it works

Free to download. Some features and themes require Omnis Premium.

Focus sessions

Silence the noise in one tap.

Pick a blocklist and a length, and the apps and websites on it stay shut while a glowing ring counts you down.

  • Blocks apps and websites, right on your phone
  • Soundscapes to focus to, and a quote to keep you going
  • Locked mode for when willpower needs backup
  • A live countdown in your notifications
Blocklists

Block exactly what distracts you.

Build lists of the sites and apps that pull you in. Adding a site blocks every page on it, and Omnis suggests the usual suspects.

  • Websites, apps, or both in one list
  • Keywords and parts of apps, like short-video feeds
  • Allowlists that block everything except what you need
Schedules

Focus that starts on its own.

Set it once and Omnis blocks your lists every weekday morning, every study evening, whenever you choose.

  • Pick the days and the hours
  • Lock a schedule so it can't be switched off while it runs
  • Only run on your work Wi-Fi, or near a place you choose
Streaks and expeditions

Keep the crystal lit.

Set a daily goal. Every day you reach it, your streak grows and your crystal grows from a shard into something brilliant.

  • A week at a glance, and a reminder before the day slips away
  • Streak freezes for the days life gets in the way
  • Expeditions: multi-day challenges with a milestone at the end
Ranks and worlds

Every minute takes you higher.

Focused minutes earn XP, and days you meet your goal count double. Follow the path from Beginner to Omnipresent.

  • Nine ranks, each with its own emblem
  • New colour themes unlocked along the way
  • New lands behind you: forests, mountains, crystal caves and the cosmos
Four worlds

The land rises with you.

Behind every screen is a living landscape. Climb the ranks and it changes beneath you.

Wellbeing

More than blocking.

Feeling scattered? Take a Reset: pick how you want to feel and breathe along with a calming singing bowl.

  • Resets to feel more settled, more alert, or ready to focus
  • Morning and evening rituals to start gently and wind down
  • Planned time offline for walks, reading, meals and people
Guided meditation

A calmer mind in ten minutes.

Spoken meditations for focus, stress and sleep, or quiet ones paced by a breathing circle and a singing bowl.

  • Calm breathing, box breathing, body scans, sleep wind-down and more
  • Ambient music and nature sounds: rain, ocean waves, forest wind
  • Choose your own length
